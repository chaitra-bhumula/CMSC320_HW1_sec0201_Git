Q1: Why might we consider using Git?

Answer: Git allows people to track changes to code over time and work with other team members without overwriting each other's work.

Q2: What field could data science be applied to? Give an Example.

Answer: A field that has a data science application is Sports. Data science is used to analyze player performance statistics and game data to inform team strategy, like in baseball's "Moneyball" approach to evaluating players.

Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: Merging keeps both branches' history and adds an extra commit to join them. Rebasing moves changes on top of the other branch, making the history look like one line instead of two.
